The lab releases two new preprints!
First, the lab introduces [GIFT, a scalable genotyping platform](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.11.717967v2)
for single-cell lineage tracing.
The second preprint is on [HSV-1 reactivation in the brain](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.29.721769v1),
implicating a population of RORB+ neurons as a potential reservoir of herpesvirus latency 
and reactivation that may trigger dementias, including Alzheimer's disease. 
Congratulations to Austin, Jacob, Benan, and Laura on the great work!
